Delete your Kerala Play account

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This page explains how a Kerala Play user can request permanent deletion of their account and the data associated with it.

Requesting deletion: Use the developer contact email shown in the App support section of Kerala Play's Google Play listing and send an account-deletion request from an email address you can access.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open Kerala Play's Google Play listing.
  2. Open App support and use the developer contact email displayed there.
  3. Use the subject Kerala Play account deletion request.
  4. Include your Kerala Play username. If you added an email address or mobile number to the account, include that information only if needed to help verify account ownership.
  5. State clearly that you want the Kerala Play account and its associated data permanently deleted.
Verification: To protect accounts from unauthorized deletion, Kerala Play may ask you to verify that you own the account before the request is completed. Never send your password in an email.

Data deleted with the account

After a valid deletion request is verified and processed, Kerala Play will remove account data associated with that account from the active service, including where applicable:

  • account and profile information such as username, optional contact information, district, avatar selection, and bio;
  • password credentials stored in protected hashed form;
  • points, levels, tasks, game progress, visited landmarks, and gameplay records tied to the account;
  • follow relationships and blocks involving the account;
  • text messages and voice messages stored by Kerala Play and associated with the account;
  • active login sessions and other account-specific service state.

Retention and processing time

Verified deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days. Limited information may be retained for longer only when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. Temporary provider backups or logs may also persist for a limited period under the service provider's normal retention practices before they are overwritten or deleted.

Deleting some data without deleting the account

Kerala Play does not currently provide a separate self-service request to delete selected stored data while keeping the account active. Supported profile fields can be edited in the app, and social controls such as unfollowing and blocking are available where provided.

Privacy information

For more information about how Kerala Play handles user data, read the Kerala Play Privacy Policy.
